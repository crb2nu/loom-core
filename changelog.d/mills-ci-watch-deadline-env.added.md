- **`LOOM_MILLS_GITLAB_HEAD_SHA_DEADLINE` / `LOOM_MILLS_GITLAB_BRANCH_PIPELINE_DEADLINE`** (`cmd/loom-mills-operator/config.go`, `cmd/loom-mills-operator/main.go`): the two `ci_watch` bounds that decide when a run stops waiting and escalates are now tunable like their neighbours, so a slow runner fleet can widen them (or a backlog drain narrow them) without a rebuild. Non-positive and unparseable values are ignored rather than wedging every poll, and the operator logs the resolved values at startup — including the silent clamp to `PollDeadline`.
