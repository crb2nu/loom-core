- Coalesce the HUD's light `agent_session_list` fetches and adapt their recv budget to the store's latency. The shuttle (3s), context-health (5s), fleet (15s, two or three fetches) and mirror monitors each scrolled the agent-context store independently; under Qdrant latency every fetch timed out at a fixed 3s, the mux discarded the reply that landed a moment later, and the next tick repeated the scroll (~2,300 wasted scrolls a day on 2026-09-13). `FleetSessions`/`ActiveSessions` now share one in-flight fetch per projection with a 2s reuse window, hand each caller its own copy, and step the budget 3s → 6s → 12s after timeouts, decaying only after a 90s quiet stretch (`internal/hud/bridge/agent_session_light.go`). `InvalidateSessionLists` forces the next tick to refetch.
