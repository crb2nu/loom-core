- **Mills research no longer fails a stage on a raw upstream 5xx** (`pkg/mills/clients/flexinfer.go`): a `status 500: Internal Server Error` from the LiteLLM/flexinfer proxy carried none of the 503 body needles, so it matched no fallback signature and returned straight out with the candidate chain untouched (2026-07-26 audit: 3 research escalations whose entire log tail was that one line). 500/502/503/504 now park the candidate in the unavailable cooldown and walk the fallback chain, surfacing `ErrModelUnavailable` — which the research stage soft-skips and the classifier maps to transient — only once every candidate is exhausted, with the real status and body preserved in the wrapped error. 4xx (and 501) stay hard failures.
