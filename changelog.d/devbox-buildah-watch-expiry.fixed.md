devbox: pod waits survive routine API-server watch expiry — a closed watch now re-syncs via Get and re-watches until the deadline instead of failing the wait. Previously a cold sandbox image build longer than one watch window (~15m) was declared failed ("watch closed for pod buildah-build-…"), its buildah pod was killed mid-build, and every Mills tests stage burned its retry budget on "sandbox image build failed" (2026-08-30 outage). Genuine deadline overruns now report "timed out after …" instead of a misleading closed-watch error.
