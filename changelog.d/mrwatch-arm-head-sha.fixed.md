- Bind the HUD mrwatch shepherd's auto-merge arming to the MR head SHA it actually observed (issue #375): the poll now carries GitLab's `sha` through `MRInfo`/`MergeRequest` into the shepherd action plan, and `ArmAutoMerge` sends it as GitLab's merge precondition (`PUT .../merge?auto_merge=true&sha=<observed>`). A branch that moves between poll and action can no longer be auto-merged unreviewed: GitLab rejects the stale-SHA arm with 409, which the shepherd audits as `head_moved` and leaves for the next poll to re-classify against the new head instead of re-arming it. An MR whose head SHA was never observed (degraded/retained snapshot) is refused outright with an audited `skipped_no_head_sha` reason, no GitLab call and no budget spent. The shepherd remains default-off behind `LOOM_MRWATCH_SHEPHERD`.
