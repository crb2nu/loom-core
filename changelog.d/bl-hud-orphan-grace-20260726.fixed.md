Fleet orphan classification no longer flaps. `fleetview.markOrphan` anchored its clock on `RegisteredAt`, so for any agent registered longer than the 120s grace a single session-list miss (slow bridge read, truncated projection) flipped it to orphan instantly — and back on the next poll, churning every fleet surface and risking false-positive orphan reaps. `JoinOpts` now carries cross-refresh "last seen with a session" evidence held by the fleet monitor: orphanhood requires the presence/session divergence to persist for the full `OrphanStaleAfter` window, and `SessionEvidence` propagates the graced verdict through the mobile handlers' re-joins so all surfaces agree.
