- **Let the store retention sweep catch up instead of backing off a day after a timeout, and record KPI snapshots every five minutes** (`pkg/mills/reconciler_retention.go`, `pkg/mills/reconciler.go`, `pkg/mills/scheduler.go`, `cmd/loom-mills-operator/main.go`): the daily retention pass had a two-minute budget and, when it timed out on a cold cache, was not retried until the next day — so the bloat that made it slow was never removed. On 2026-09-08 the production store was 497 MB with 82k bookkeeping events past the 14-day retention and ~130k KPI snapshots past 90 days still present, cold boots spent minutes warming report rollups, and the 5000-row delete batches held the write lock past `busy_timeout`, surfacing as `database is locked (SQLITE_BUSY)` on the ledger writer. A timed-out pass now retries every 10 minutes until one completes, batches are 1000 rows, and the scheduler records KPI snapshots at most every 5 minutes (`Scheduler.KPIRecordInterval`, zero keeps the per-tick behaviour) instead of every minute, cutting `kpi_snapshots` growth from ~4.3k to ~860 rows a day.
