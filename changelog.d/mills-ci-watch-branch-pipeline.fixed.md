- **Bound `ci_watch` on a merge request whose branch pipeline never appears** (`pkg/mills/clients/gitlab.go`, `pkg/mills/pipeline/dispatcher.go`, `pkg/mills/pipeline/error_class.go`, `pkg/mills/pipeline/runner.go`): the with-a-head twin of the head-SHA wedge. When GitLab publishes the MR head but no `push` pipeline ever exists for it — `workflow:rules` that admit only `merge_request_event`, CI disabled, a deleted pipeline — `PollPipeline` now fails on a dedicated `GitLabConfig.BranchPipelineDeadline` (default 10m, clamped to `PollDeadline`, reset by a repush) with the new `ErrBranchPipelineUnavailable`, classified `config` ahead of `ErrPipelinePollTimeout`. The escalation names the pipelines that DO exist for the SHA and points at the project's CI configuration, instead of replaying `MR <n> branch pipeline pending` through the full 90-minute watch cap and reporting a stall on a pipeline that never existed.
