- **Mills merge stage now auto-recovers a stale-head GitLab 405** instead of escalating it to a human: when `PUT .../merge` returns exactly 405 on a still-open MR whose head pipeline is not owned by the superseding-pipeline path, and GitLab itself reports the MR as `mergeable` or blocked only on CI, `pkg/mills/clients/gitlab.go` closes and reopens the MR once per stage attempt so `head_pipeline` and mergeability recompute against the branch's existing green pipeline, then retries the merge. The cycle is refused unless a successful pipeline already exists for the CI-authorized SHA, re-asserts the head-SHA authorization fence before and after, reopens on a context detached from the merge deadlines so an expiring clock cannot strand the MR closed, and emits `pipeline.merge.recovery405` (wired in `pkg/mills/pipeline/runner.go`) before mutating anything. A 405 that survives recovery escalates as before, now carrying the original error plus what the recovery observed.
