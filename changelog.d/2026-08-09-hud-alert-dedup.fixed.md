- **Stop duplicate alert cards for persisting pipeline conditions** (`internal/hud/alerting/engine.go`): the alert engine re-fired every cooldown window while a condition persisted — a stuck pipeline minted a new card (and a new dispatcher notification) every 15 minutes, stacking 9 identical "Pipeline stuck" cards for one zombie pipeline. All three rules now dedup fire-side against an open (unresolved) card: stuck and failed key on rule+pipeline, consecutive-failures keys on rule+project+ref (the streak's identity). The open stuck card's message refreshes in place so its duration stays honest, and a condition recurring after resolution still fires a fresh card.
