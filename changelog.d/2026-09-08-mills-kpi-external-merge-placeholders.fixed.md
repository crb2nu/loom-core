- **Stop counting merge-queue placeholder rows as merged pipeline runs** (`pkg/mills/kpi_writer.go`, `pkg/mills/store/dao_pipeline.go`): the serial merge queue records a terminal `done` `pipeline_runs` row for every external fleet candidate at enqueue time, before any rebase and regardless of a later eviction. Those rows were counted by every pipeline KPI — on 2026-09-08 the 1d window reported 13 merged runs of which 7 were placeholders, five for candidates the queue had evicted for `ci_red`, `ci_timeout`, or `rebase_conflict` — inflating `auto_merge_rate`, `council_roi`, and the `pipeline_merged_real` floor the circuit breaker reads while diluting `cost_per_merged_change_usd`, and an evicted candidate could advance `last_merge_at` in `/api/mills/status`. Every `pipeline_*` counter now excludes the `external_merge` template; MRs the queue actually landed for fleet producers are reported under a new `mergequeue_external_merged` key computed from settled queue rows. `KPIWriter.Record` also records each window independently, so a 30d scan exhausting the KPI budget no longer drops the 1d snapshot that drives status, the HUD, and the breaker.
