- **Fail a spawn whose agent stream aborted, even with nothing left in flight** (`internal/hud/spawn.go`, `internal/hud/spawn_codex_parser.go`, `internal/hud/spawn_gemini_parser.go`, `internal/hud/spawn_claude_parser.go`): the stream-abort reason was consulted only after the open-tool-call gate, so a Codex stream that emitted `{"type":"error"}`, completed no turn, and had no calls open reached `completeSpawn` as a success — the case the Codex error handler already describes as "a spawn completed without producing work". A recorded abort reason is now a verdict on its own. Gemini and Claude gained the same reporting: Gemini surfaces its last error-severity `error` event when no terminal `result` arrived, and Claude — whose stream-json has no fatal event — surfaces the API-retry storm that preceded a stream stopping without its terminal `result`. The veto requires positive evidence, so a quiet truncation still falls through to the existing open-tool-call rule and a terminal `result` (including `error_max_turns`) keeps its previous outcome.
