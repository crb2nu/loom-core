- Fixed Mills retry classification for keyed-spawn rollout collisions in `pkg/mills/pipeline/spawn_class.go` and `pkg/mills/pipeline/error_class.go`: a fleet roll that kills a spawn pod mid-start (`pod creation failed: … watch closed for pod …`) or leaves the keyed pod terminating when the stage re-dispatches (HUD 400 `preflight keyed spawn … runtime identity conflict`) now classifies as a retryable, auto-requeue-eligible spawn-transport transient with a distinct `spawn-pod-lifecycle` / `spawn-runtime-identity-conflict` reason, instead of escalating as `class=code` against the diff.
