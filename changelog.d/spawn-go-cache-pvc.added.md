- **Shared Go build/module cache for spawn pods** (`internal/hud/spawn.go`, `internal/devbox/backend/backend.go`, `internal/devbox/backend/k8s_objects.go`, `k8s/base/servers/mobile-hud/{deployment,spawn-go-cache-pvc}.yaml`): every spawn clones fresh into an emptyDir, so `go build`/`go test` recompiled the whole dependency tree per attempt — on 2026-07-26 those cold compiles ate 20+ minutes of 25-minute spawn deadlines (17/73 failed stage-attempts were deadline kills, most after the agent had already authored its change; the pr_self_review stage ran a 57.6% error rate). New opt-in `SPAWN_GO_CACHE_PVC` names an RWX claim the k8s backend mounts at `/gocache` in every spawn pod (generic `StartOpts.CachePVCs` plumbing), with `GOCACHE=/gocache/go-build` and `GOMODCACHE=/gocache/gomod` injected into the pod env so dependency compiles are paid once per fleet instead of once per spawn. Unset = byte-identical legacy pods; non-k8s substrates ignore it. Ships enabled with a 20Gi Longhorn RWX claim (`devbox/spawn-go-cache`) — both cache types are concurrency-safe (content-addressed build cache; lock-filed module cache), and corruption recovery is just deleting the PVC (worst case: today's cold-compile behavior).
