- **Refuse to re-drive a merge-queue head already evicted for a rebase conflict** (`pkg/mills/mergequeue/external.go`, `pkg/mills/store/dao_merge_queue.go`, `cmd/loom-mills-operator/handlers_merge_queue.go`): external producers could re-enqueue the same MR head after every target-branch move (GitLab reports mergeability as unchecked for a moment after `main` advances, so the mrwatch shepherd re-armed a stale canary MR with known conflicts each time), and the queue obediently rebased and evicted it again. `ExternalEnqueuer` now consults the MR's latest settled queue row and answers `conflicted` (HTTP 409, nothing enqueued) when that row is a `rebase_conflict` eviction of the offered head; a new head, a merged history, or any other eviction reason still admits the candidate.
